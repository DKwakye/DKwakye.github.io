About

I am currently a Research Assistant at HeiGIT (Heidelberg Institute for Geoinformation Technology). I obtained my bachelor’s degree in Geomatic Engineering from Kwame Nkrumah University of Science and Technology, Ghana in 2016 and completed the English-taught Geodetic Engineering Master’s programme at the University of Bonn, Germany in 2022, majoring in Geoinformation and Spatial Development with a minor in Mobile Sensing and Robotics.

Researcher & GIS Engineer

My general research area is Computational Sustainability, with a focus on the quality and analysis of volunteered geographic information such as OpenStreetMap.
