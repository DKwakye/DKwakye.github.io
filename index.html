About

I am a PhD researcher in the Structural Mechanics and Monitoring group at the Institute of Structural Engineering (IBK), ETH Zurich, working within the Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Network TWEED (Training Wind Energy Experts on Digitalisation). My doctoral work develops a robust framework for generating knowledge graphs that integrate heterogeneous wind-energy data so it can be reused across disciplines.

Researcher in knowledge engineering & geoinformatics

My interests sit at the intersection of computational sustainability, semantic web technologies and the quality of open geospatial data.
